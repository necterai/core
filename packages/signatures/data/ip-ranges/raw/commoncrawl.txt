class="uui-max-width-small"><div class="paragraph">Common Crawl is a non-profit foundation founded with the goal of democratizing access to web information by producing and maintaining an open repository of web crawl data that is universally accessible and analyzable by anyone.<br/><br/>Enabling free access to web crawl data encourages collaboration and interdisciplinary research, as organizations, academia, and non-profits can work together to address complex challenges. Collaborating using Open Data accelerates progress and helps find solutions to pressing global issues, such as climate change, public health, and social equality.<br/><br/>By embracing Open Data, we promote an inclusive and thriving knowledge ecosystem, where the collective intelligence of the global community can lead to transformative discoveries and positive societal impact.<br/><br/>CCBot identifies itself in its <code>UserAgent</code> string as:</div><div class="code-multi"><strong class="bold-text-9">CCBot/2.0 (</strong><a target="_blank" href="https://commoncrawl.org/faq/"><strong class="bold-text-9">https://commoncrawl.org/faq/</strong></a><strong class="bold-text-9">)</strong></div><div class="paragraph">To prevent Common Crawl from crawling your website, include the following in your <span class="code-string">robots.txt</span>:</div><div class="code-multi"><strong class="bold-text-9">User-agent: CCBot<br/> Disallow: /</strong></div><div class="uui-space-small-2"></div><div class="paragraph"><strong>Please note that we are aware of crawlers falsely identifying themselves as CCBot. We recommend verifying UserAgent strings to ensure authenticity.<br/>‍</strong><br/>CCBot is now run on dedicated IP address ranges with reverse DNS (except over IPv6 where reverse DNS is not yet supported.) This allows webmasters to verify whether a logged request stems from the real CCBot, for example:<strong><code>‍</code></strong></div><div class="code-multi"><strong class="bold-text-9"><code>$&gt;
